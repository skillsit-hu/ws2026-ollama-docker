Allow me to describe, based on Thomas's summary and what was said at the latest expert meeting, my interpretation of the full picture regarding the use of local AI models in Module F.

In the WorldSkills Shanghai 2026 competition, in the Web Development category, AI will be used for the first time this year.

1. The central servers will not have GPUs with dedicated VRAM, so a central LLM service cannot be implemented.
2. The workstations are equipped with GTX 4070 GPUs, so they are suitable for running smaller LLM models.
3. Ollama will be installed on the workstations.
4. Competitors may use either of the two ~4B models available on Ollama, exclusively in Module F, without limitation.
5. Module F belongs to the following two WSOS categories: a) design implementation b) frontend development.
6. On the familiarization day, an even smaller model will be available for testing and trial purposes.
7. Competitors will only learn about the two 4B models available in Module F during the module itself.
8. Ollama will be pre-installed, but the information needed to download and run the models will be provided to competitors in the form of a handout.
